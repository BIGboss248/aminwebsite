# 0001. Bilingual i18n and Global Directionality (LTR/RTL)

To serve both global talent recruiters and domestic Iranian clients, the site implements full first-class bilingual support (`en` and `fa`). English defaults to LTR with modern sans-serif typography, and Persian switches to RTL with the Vazirmatn typeface, with directional layout flipping handled globally at the root container and component levels.
